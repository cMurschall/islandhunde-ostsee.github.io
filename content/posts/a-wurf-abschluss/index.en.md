Our A litter is now complete. The six puppies developed healthily and appropriately in their first weeks and have since moved to their new families. During their upbringing, the focus was on a calm, structured environment, good socialization within the pack, and gentle introductions to everyday situations on the farm.

Regular veterinary checkups and weight monitoring accompanied the puppies' development. Short outings, contact with children and visitors, and exposure to different surfaces and environments were part of their routine. All puppies were curious and open throughout.
