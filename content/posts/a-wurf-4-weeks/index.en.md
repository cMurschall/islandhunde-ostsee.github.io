### 🐾 Our Puppies Are 4 Weeks Old! 🐾

Time flies so fast! Our little Icelandic Sheepdog puppies are already **four weeks old**, curious, playful, and full of excitement.

Their first little adventures in the garden are now part of the daily routine. Still a bit wobbly, but getting more confident every day, they explore the world around them and carefully inspect every single blade of grass.

They also play more and more with each other, growl, and tumble around. The puppies are learning dog language quickly. Between all the play, there is still plenty of time for sleep and cuddles, preferably close together or next to mom.

They have already tried their **first solid meals** with great enthusiasm, and it seems they love it! 🍽️ Each day, our puppy world becomes a little livelier and a lot cuter.
